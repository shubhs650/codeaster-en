# POST_ELEM operator

## 1 Purpose

Compute quantities over all or part of the structure. The quantities computed correspond to particular calculation options of the modeling type assigned.

The options currently available are:

- mass, inertias and position of the center of gravity,
- potential energy,
- kinetic energy,
- dissipated energy (DKTG elements),
- work of the external forces,
- indicators of loss of load proportionality in elastoplasticity,
- limit load,
- Weibull stress,
- growth rate of a spherical cavity (Rice - Tracey),
- elastic energy and total energy,
- area of a hole in a 2D mesh,
- integral and mean of a field,
- surface/volume distribution of the value of a field component,
- spatial extrema of a list of field components,
- norm of a field.

The object returned is a table.

## 3 Operands

The operands MODELE, CHAM_MATER and CARA_ELEM are optional if RESULTAT is given, and required if CHAM_GD is given.

### 3.1 Operand MODELE

```text
◊  MODELE = mo,
```

Name of the model on which the option is computed. The model name is optional because it is held in the `resultat` data structure.

### 3.2 Operand CHAM_MATER

```text
◊  CHAM_MATER = chmater,
```

Material field associated with the model mo; optional because it is held in the `resultat` data structure.

### 3.3 Operand CARA_ELEM

```text
◊  CARA_ELEM = carac,
```

The element properties carac are needed if the model contains structural elements (beam, plate, shell or discrete elements); optional because they are held in the `resultat` data structure.

### 3.4 Operands NUME_COUCHE / NIVE_COUCHE

```text
◊  NUME_COUCHE = nume,
```

For a multilayer material, an integer between 1 and the number of layers, needed to give the layer in which the elementary calculation is done. By convention, layer 1 is the bottom layer (along the normal to the element) for mechanical shell or thermal shell elements.

```text
◊  NIVE_COUCHE =
```

For the layer nume given by NUME_COUCHE, gives the ordinate at which the elementary calculation is done:

| 'INF' bottom ordinate of the layer | (inner skin), |
|---|---|
| 'SUP' top ordinate of the layer | (outer skin), |
| 'MOY' mean ordinate of the layer | (mid-surface, the default). |

### 3.5 Operand MODE_FOURIER

```text
◊  MODE_FOURIER =
```

Number of the FOURIER harmonic: an integer greater than or equal to zero (default = 0).

### 3.6 Operand GEOMETRIE

<!-- page 5 -->

```text
♦  GEOMETRIE =  /   'INITIALE',         [DEFAUT]
                /   'DEFORMEE',
```

Says whether the calculation uses the initial geometry or the deformed shape. In the latter case, a displacement field must be given with CHAM_GD or RESULTAT.

### 3.7 Operands CHAM_GD / RESULTAT

The options ENER_POT and ENER_CIN are computed from an existing nodal or element field, or one extracted from a `resultat`.

#### 3.7.1 Operand CHAM_GD

```text
♦  /  CHAM_GD = cham,
```

Name of a field (for the options ENER_POT and ENER_CIN).

For the option ENER_POT, give a displacement field or a temperature field (see [§3.9]). For the option ENER_CIN, give a velocity field (without a frequency) or a displacement field and a frequency (see [§3.9]).

#### 3.7.2 Operand RESULTAT

```text
/  RESULTAT = resu,
```

Name of a result object of type `evol_elas`, `evol_ther`, `mode_meca`, `evol_noli`, `mult_elas`, `fourier_elas` or `dyna_trans`.

```text
OptionENER_POT: evol_elas  ,  evol_ther  , mode_meca  ,  mult_elas  ,
                fourier_elas evol_noli oudyna_trans.
OptionENER_CIN: mode_meca  , evol_elas , evol_ther  , evol_noli  , ou
                dyna_trans .
OptionENER_ELASetENER_TOTALE:evol_noli, evol_elas
```

##### 3.7.2.1 Operands TOUT_ORDRE / NUME_ORDRE / NUME_MODE / LIST_ORDRE / NOEUD_CMP / FREQ /

```text
LIST_FREQ/INST /LIST_INST/PRECISION/CRITERE
```

See [U4.71.00].

### 3.8 Keyword MASS_INER

#### 3.8.1 Purpose

Factor keyword that computes the mass, the inertias and the center of gravity. This option computes on each element the following characteristics: ( being the density defined in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01] by ELAS or ELAS_FO). <!-- equation -->

m=∫dv Mass: v <!-- equation -->

1 1 1 Center of gravity: x = ∫ xdv;y = ∫ ydv;z = ∫ zdv G m G m G m v v v <!-- equation -->

Inertia tensor at the center of gravity G in the global coordinate system in which the mesh is described: I G=∫ y−y 2 z−z 2 dv I G=∫x−x y−y dv xx G G xy G G v v I G=∫ x−x 2 z−z 2 dv I G=∫x−x z−z dv yy G G xz G G v v I G=∫ x−x 2 y−y 2 dv I G=∫y−y z−z dv zz G G yz G G v v <!-- equation -->

It then computes by "summation" the quantities for the whole structure.

The principal inertia tensor, made of the eigenvalues of the inertia matrix, is computed, together with the 3 Euler angles of the principal inertia coordinate system. These components are named in the table:

```text
IX_PRIN_G IY_PRIN_G IZ_PRIN_G ALPHA BETA GAMMA.
```

#### 3.8.2 Syntax

```text
|  MASS_INER =_F(
   ♦  /   TOUT    =' OUI',
      /   GROUP_MA=l grma,          [l_gr_maille]
   ◊  ORIG_INER= (xp, yp [,zp]),    [l_R]
          ),
```

Simple keywords: (see [§2])

```text
◊  MODELE =  mo,                 [modele]
◊  CHAM_MATER = c hmater,        [cham_mater]
◊  CARA_ELEM =  c arac,          [cara_elem]
◊  |   NUME_COUCHE =
   |   NIVE_COUCHE =
◊  MODE_FOURIER =
◊  GEOMETRIE    =
   ♦   /  CHAM_GD =
       /  RESULTAT =
```

Note For the factor keyword MASS_INER , the model and the material field are required if RESULTAT and CHAM_GD are absent.

#### 3.8.3 Operands

```text
♦  GEOMETRIE    =   /  'INTIALE',      [DEFAUT]
                    /  'DEFORMEE',
```

Says whether the calculation uses the initial geometry or the deformed shape. In the latter case, a displacement field must be given with CHAM_GD or RESULTAT.

```text
♦  /  TOUT    = 'OUI',
```

On the whole structure.

```text
/  GROUP_MA =    lgrma,
```

On a list of element groups. If the list holds more than one element group, a row is added to the output table with the result on the union of the elements of the groups, in addition to the result per group. This new row corresponds to the element group ‘UNION_GROUP_MA’.

```text
◊  ORIG_INER = (xp, yp [,zp]),         [l_R]
```

Point about which the inertia tensor is computed.

The inertia tensor at the point P with coordinates (xp, yp, zp) is obtained from the inertia tensor at the center of gravity G, the mass m of the structure and the coordinates of G by the formulas:

I P=I Gmx2 xx xx PG I P=I Gmy2 yy yy PG I P=I Gmz2 zz zz PG I P=I Gmx y xy xy PG PG I P=I Gmx z xz xz PG PG I P=I Gmy z yz yz PG PG with x =x −x PG G P y =y −y PG G P z =z −z PG G P <!-- equation -->

### 3.26 Operand VOLUMOGRAMME

#### 3.26.1 Purpose

Computes the surface or volume distribution of the value of a field component at different times over a domain given by the keywords TOUT or GROUP_MA. It produces a table holding:

- the bounds of the intervals, defined by the minimum and maximum values of the component and the number of intervals wanted,
- the percentage of the structure corresponding to each interval.

Remark The number of intervals is given by the user; the bounds are set by Code_Aster as follows: let n be the number of intervals, and Vmin and Vmax the extreme values of the component. Let p=Vmax−Vmin/n be the length of each interval. The intervals are: [Vmin,Vminp],[Vminp,Vmin2p],...,[Vminn−1p,Vmax] <!-- equation -->

#### 3.26.2 Syntax

```text
|  VOLUMOGRAMME =_F(
   ♦  / TOUT       =   'OUI',
      / GROUP_MA   =   grma,        [gr_maille]
   ◊  TYPE_MAILLE  =   /'2D',
                       /'3D',
   ◊  NOM_CHAM     =   champ,       [Kn]
   ♦  NOM_CMP      =   cmp,         [Kn]
   ◊  BORNES       =   (bmin, bmax),[l_R]
   ◊  NORME        =   /'RELATIF'   [defaut]
                       /'ABSOLU'
   ◊  |   NB_INTERV    = nb_int     [I]
      |   SEUIL        = seuil      [R]
          )
```

Simple keywords: (see [§2])

```text
   ◊  MODELE       =   mo,       [modele]
   ◊  CHAM_MATER   =   chmater,  [cham_mater]
   ◊  CARA_ELEM    =   carac,    [cara_elem]
   ♦  /   CHAM_GD  =   cham,     [cham_gd]
      /   RESULTAT =   resu,     [resultat]
# sélection des instants de calcul

```

```text
♦  /  TOUT      =  ' OUI',
```

On the whole structure.

```text
/  GROUP_MA  =  l grma,
```

On an element group.

```text

```

Filters by element dimension.

```text

```


```text
◊  NORME         =  /'RELATIF'   [defaut]
```

With NORME='RELATIF', the results shown in the DISTRIBUTION column of the table produced are percentages (between 0 and 100) of the total volume computed. With NORME='ABSOLU', this normalization is not done and the results shown are the actual volumes, interval by interval, in the unit of the mesh.

### 3.27 Operand NORME

#### 3.27.1 Purpose

Computes the norm of a field over a domain given by the keywords TOUT, GROUP_MA, for continuous-medium elements only. Currently the L norm and the 2 Frobenius norm can be computed (see the documentation of CALC_CHAMP). It produces a table holding the norm of the field. <!-- equation -->

#### 3.27.2 Syntax

```text
|  NORME =_F (
   ◊  TYPE_NORM     =  |'L2',    [Kn]
                        |'FROBENIUS'
   ♦  / TOUT        =  'OUI',
      / GROUP_MA    =  lgrma,    [l_gr_maille]
   ◊  TYPE_MAILLE   =  / '2D',   [Kn]
                       / '3D',
   ♦  NOM_CHAM      =  champ,    [Kn]
   #Si CHAM_GD est renseigné
   ◊  COEF_MULT     =  coef,     [l_R]
          )
```

On a list of element groups.

The fields cham accepted are the fields DEPL, TEMP, NEUT_R, FLUX, SIEF and EPSI.

```text

```

Gives a list of real coefficients to weight the components of a field of type NEUT_R . If this list is not given, the coefficients are set to 1. If the list is shorter than the number of components of the field, it is completed with zero values.

## 4 Example

The following example computes global quantities on a dynamic model of a reactor building. The model includes: the outer containment, the inner containment, the internal structures, the vessel pit.

Half of the building is modeled with beam elements and with discrete elements representing the soil supports, the added masses and the connections between nodes.

On this beam model the operator POST_ELEM computes:

- the mass of the structure,
- the coordinates of the center of gravity,
- the inertia tensor,
- the potential energy of some modes and its distribution in the structure,
- the kinetic energy of some modes and its distribution in the structure.

### 4.1 Mass, center of gravity and inertias

- for the whole structure (TOUT = 'OUI')
- for the element group holding the beams ( GROUP_MA = 'pou_d_t' )
- for the element group holding the soil supports ( GROUP_MA = 'liai_sol')
- for the element group holding the added masses ( GROUP_MA = 'masses' )
- for the element group holding the connections between nodes ( GROUP_MA = 'liai_noe' )

Command

```text
massestr = POST_ELEM ( MODELE=   stickmod,
                       CHAM_MATER = chmater,
                       CARA_ELEM = caraelem,
                       MASS_INER = _F(GROUP_MA=( 'pou_d_t', 'liai_sol',
                                                 'masses', 'liai_noe' ),
                       TOUT= 'OUI',),
                       TITRE='masse, centre de gravite et inerties de la
structure');
```

### 4.2 Potential energy of modes 1, 2 and 7

- for the whole structure (TOUT = 'OUI')
- for the element group holding the beams ( GROUP_MA = 'pou_d_t' )
- for the element group holding the soil supports ( GROUP_MA = 'liai_sol')
- for the element group holding the added masses ( GROUP_MA = 'masses' )

```text
enerpot = POST_ELEM (  RESULTAT = modes,

                                                     'masses', 'liai_noe'),
```
