# POST_RCCM operator

## 1 Purpose

Check criteria of the RCC-M. These include:

- some ZE200 fatigue criteria, as post-processing of piping calculations (with or without environmental effect),
- the B3600 fatigue criteria, as post-processing of piping calculations,
- the B3600 equivalent-moment criterion, as post-processing of piping calculations,
- the B3200 level 0 and level A criteria (with or without environmental effect), as post-processing of calculations on 2D or 3D structures;.

The level 0 criteria aim to protect the equipment against excessive deformation and against elastic, elastoplastic or plastic instability. The level A criteria aim to protect the equipment against progressive deformation and fatigue. The fatigue damage can also be computed taking into account the effects of the PWR environment (recent change to the RCC-M by AFCEN).

As input of the command POST_RCCM, give:

  - either stress tables on an analysis segment, from finite element calculations

( TYPE_RESU_MECA=’EVOLUTION’ and TYPE_RESU_MECA=’B3200’ );

  - or the result of calculations on a piping line (TYPE_RESU_MECA=’B3600’).
  - or the result of calculations on a piping line and stress tables

from finite element calculations (TYPE_RESU_MECA=’ZE200a’ and TYPE_RESU_MECA=’ZE200b’);

It produces a data structure of type table.

Before a first use, reading the reference document [R7.04.03] and the advice document [U2.09.03] is strongly recommended.

### 3.4 Types of results: keyword TYPE_RESU_MECA

Five types of post-processing are available in POST_RCCM: they follow the rules of different paragraphs of the RCC-M: B3600, B3200, ZE200.

Results of type RCC-M B3600 for piping: 'B3600' With the option FATIGUE, this type of result is for post-processing mechanical calculations ( MECA_STATIQUE , STAT_NON_LINE , COMB_SISM_MODAL ) on a piping line. The fatigue calculation follows the rules of paragraph B3600. Several situations can be defined, but in a single group. Seismic situations can be taken into account.

With the option MOMENT_EQUIVALENT, this type of result is for post-processing mechanical calculations ( MECA_STATIQUE , STAT_NON_LINE , DYNA_VIBRA, DYNA_NON_LINE ) on a piping line. This option computes the maximum equivalent moments on the line, with the formula of paragraph B3600: MEQ=√(MT2+M FY2+MFZ2) The table produced holds the time maxima of the components MT, MFY, MFZ and MEQ, together with the curvilinear abscissa of the node concerned.

Results of type RCC-M B3200: 'EVOLUTION' and 'B3200' 'EVOLUTION' is for post-processing one or more thermomechanical calculations (MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE) on a 2D or 3D modeling type. The results are passed as stress tables, extracted on the analysis segment or segments. The criteria available are:

<!-- page 17 -->

  - for regular zones (away from geometric singularities): the level 0 criteria

(option PM_PB), level A criteria excluding fatigue (option SN) and fatigue criteria (option FATIGUE_ZH210) on segments;

  - for geometric singularities: calculation of the initiation factor (option AMORCAGE) on a circular cut line around the singularity. This option suits cases with few situations to study, or cases where the initiation factor must be determined.

'B3200', on the other hand, suits calculations on a component subjected to many situations, possibly spread over several operating groups. An earthquake can be taken into account, as well as transition situations and sharing groups. Environmental fatigue resistance can also be assessed with this option.

The loads can be given in several forms. This type of result may therefore need the prior calculation of:

- the stresses due to the thermal transients considered, which must be extracted on the analysis segment ( RESU_THER ) ,
- the stresses due to pressure loads: as a transient ( RESU_PRES ) or as a unit load with two pressures for the stabilized states ( RESU_MECA_UNIT, PRES_A and PRES_B ),
- the stresses due to loads from forces and moments:
  - as a transient ( RESU_MECA )
  - for unit loads (unit global forces and moments applied at the boundaries of the model) with two torsors for the stabilized states (CHAR_ETAT_A and CHAR_ETAT_B). These forces can be either computed with code_aster , or taken from the OAR database,
  - for unit loads to which a torsor is applied, this torsor is computed by interpolation between two torsors ( CHAR_ETAT_ A and CHAR_ETAT_ B ) that correspond to the temperatures TEMP_A , and TEMP_B , using the temperature during the situation TABL_TEMP.

The criteria available are the level 0 criteria (option PM_PB ), level A criteria excluding fatigue (option SN ), fatigue (option FATIGUE ) and environmental fatigue (option EFAT) on segments.

Results of type RCC-M ZE200: 'ZE200a' and 'ZE200b'

The RCC-M ZE200 method is a mixed method that combines B3200 and B3600. This type of result is therefore for post-processing mechanical calculations on a piping line according to B3600, combined with thermal transients for 'ZE200a' . The method 'ZE200b' also processes the stresses due to pressure as a transient. These options suit calculations on piping subjected to many situations, possibly spread over several operating groups. An earthquake can be taken into account, as well as transition situations and sharing groups. Environmental fatigue resistance can also be assessed with this option.

## 4 Operands specific to results of type B3200

### 4.1 Preliminaries

From several results of mechanical calculations on a component, the RCC-M level 0 and level A criteria are computed. The loads can be given in several forms. This type of result may therefore need the prior calculation of:

- the stresses due to the thermal transients considered, which must be extracted on the analysis segment ( RESU_THER ) ,
- the stresses due to pressure loads: as a transient ( RESU_PRES ) or as a unit load with two pressures for the stabilized states ( RESU_MECA_UNIT, PRES_A and PRES_B ),
- the stresses due to loads from forces and moments:
  - as a transient ( RESU_MECA )
  - for unit loads (unit global forces and moments applied at the boundaries of the model) with two torsors for the stabilized states (CHAR_ETAT_A and CHAR_ETAT_B). These forces can be either computed with code_aster , or taken from the OAR database. The coordinate system used for the 2D or 3D calculation must be consistent with the one in which the global forces from the beam calculation are expressed.
  - for unit loads to which a torsor is applied, this torsor is computed by interpolation between two torsors ( CHAR_ETAT_ A and CHAR_ETAT_ B ) that correspond to the temperatures TEMP_A , and TEMP_B , using the temperature during the situation TABL_TEMP.

#### 4.1.1 Option PM_PB

Option that computes the level 0 criteria, which aim to protect the equipment against excessive deformation, plastic instability, and elastic and elastoplastic instability. These criteria need the calculation of the equivalent stresses of membrane Pm, local membrane Pl, bending Pb, and membrane plus bending Pm+Pb.

#### 4.1.2 Option SN

Option that computes the level A criteria (excluding fatigue), which aim to protect the equipment against progressive deformation. They need the calculation of the range of linearized stress at a point, written Sn. Under some conditions, this option also computes Sn* and the thermal ratcheting (if the factor keyword RESU_THER is present).

Remark:

With the option ‘ SN ’, the calculation is done without combining the situations defined: each situation is processed in turn. To get the quantities with combination between each situation, use the option ‘ FATIGUE ’.

#### 4.1.3 Option FATIGUE

The fatigue calculations follow RCCM B3200 on the analysis segment. Two fictitious transients are identified to combine two situations with each other [R7.04.03].

#### 4.1.4 Option EFAT

The environmental fatigue calculations (option ‘EFAT’) follow RCCM B3200 on the analysis segment. Two fictitious transients are identified to combine two situations with each other. Then the effects of the PWR environment on the usage factor are taken into account. Add the factor keyword 'ENVIRONNEMENT' and the keywords 'O_ETOILE' and 'TABL_TEMP' under each occurrence of the factor keyword 'SITUATION' [R7.04.03].

## 9 Operands specific to results of type EVOLUTION

For a precise description of the calculations done by these options, see the document [R7.04.03] and the user notice [U2.09.03].

The material properties needed to compute the criteria are defined with the command DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01]. The computed values and the limit values are stored in the table tabl_post_rccm, which is printed with the command IMPR_TABLE [U4.91.03].

Remark on using temperature-dependent material properties:

For the post-processing, the RCC-M B rules require the material properties, such as Sm, at the maximum temperature of the transients.

The operator DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01] does however allow material properties to be defined as functions of temperature (factor keyword RCCM_FO) . With the option ' EVOLUTION ', the user must define the material properties for the post-processing only with the factor keyword RCCM of the command DEFI_MATERIAU . Indeed, to use the option ' EVOLUTION' , the user gives only stresses as input data; the thermal evolution is ignored even when it exists in the earlier calculation steps. With the option ' EVOLUTION ', using temperature-dependent properties therefore causes an execution error.

The analysis is a post-processing of one or more thermomechanical calculations (MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE) on a 2D or 3D modeling type. The results are passed as stress tables, extracted on the analysis segment or segments. These stress tables can be created with the commands POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE. The possible calculation options are:

1) level 0 criteria with the option PM_PB ; 2) level A criteria (excluding fatigue) with the option SN ; 3) fatigue criteria (also level A) with the option FATIGUE_ZH210 ; 4) initiation criteria in singular zones with the option AMORCAGE.

The first three options can be called alone or together. The last option can only be called alone: it needs stresses collected on a circular cut line around the geometric singularity, whereas the other options are for segments crossing the structure.

This option suits cases with few situations to study. Seismic situations cannot be taken into account.

#### 9.1.1 Operand MATER

```text
♦     MATER =     mat
```

The material holding the properties used by POST_RCCM, defined under the keyword RCCM of DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01].

Remark on fatigue curves:

For small stress amplitudes, the question of extending the fatigue curve can arise: 6 for example, for the RCCM fatigue curves beyond 10 cycles, the corresponding stress, 180 MPa, is considered as the endurance limit; that is, any stress below 180 MPa must give a zero usage factor, or an infinite number of allowable cycles.

<!-- page 43 -->

Recall that the fatigue curve is defined in the material properties (keyword FATIGUE / WOHLER ) as the number of cycles to failure as a function of the stress half-amplitude Salt. Small stress amplitudes therefore correspond to the extension of the curve to the left. Several cases can arise, depending on the type of extension chosen in DEFI_FONCTION :

- if PROL_GAUCHE = 'EXCLU' or 'CONSTANT', the calculation assumes that the first value of the Wohler curve given is the endurance limit of the material. In other words, any alternating stress smaller than the first value given in the DEFI_FONCTION gives a zero usage factor. The method used here therefore matches the notion of endurance limit;
- if PROL_GAUCHE = 'LINEAIRE', the curve is extended linearly. Note that the extension is not done in logarithmic coordinates, so usage factors that are not negligible can be obtained even for small values of Salt. To return to the notion of endurance limit, it is then recommended to add to the definition of the curve the number of allowable cycles for a very small value of Salt (computed by hand, for example by interpolation with a power law).

#### 9.1.2 Operand SY_MAX

```text
◊     SY_MAX      =     symax,
```

Conventional yield strength at the maximum temperature reached during the cycle. This operand is only used to compute thermal ratcheting (see § 9.1.4.2). If the yield strength SY_MAX is not defined, the value defined under the operand SY_02 of the keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01] is used; if that operand is not defined either, thermal ratcheting cannot be computed.

#### 9.1.1 Operand AXIS

```text
◊     AXIS  =  / ‘NON’   [DEFAUT]
              / ‘OUI’
```

Flag that declares the modeling type axisymmetric. If this operand takes the value ‘OUI’, the equivalent membrane stress Pm and bending stress Pb are computed by an algorithm designed for axisymmetric models. Because the mass is distributed unevenly in the radial direction, this algorithm introduces a corrective shift of the mean line in the stress linearization formulation.

#### 9.1.2 Operand RAYON_MOYEN

```text
◊     RHO_MOYEN   =  rho, (défaut : -1.)
```

Topological curvature of the mean line at its intersection with the extraction segment, in the XOY plane of the axisymmetric model. The default value -1 means that the segment being processed is locally straight. Otherwise, the curvature must be strictly positive.

This operand is taken into account if the operand AXIS = ‘OUI’ .

#### 9.1.3 Option PM_PB

<!-- chunk 25 fr=630586328486 method=machine source=session:task-5.2 on=2026-10-30 -->

Option that computes the level 0 criteria, which aim to protect the equipment against excessive deformation, plastic instability, and elastic and elastoplastic instability. These criteria need the calculation of the equivalent stresses of membrane Pm, local membrane Pl, bending Pb, and membrane plus bending Pm+Pb.

The operands needed are MATER, the stress table TABL_RESU_MECA (built by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE after the mechanical calculation on the post-processing location) and possibly the stress table TABL_SIGM_THER built from a calculation with the thermal load alone.

<!-- page 44 -->

The calculation points are the two ends of the analysis segment. If several extraction segments were used to define the same stress table, the calculation is done for each of them in turn.

The limit values are Sm and 1,5*Sm, Sm being the allowable stress, a function of the material and the temperature, given by the keyword SM of the keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01].

Remarks:

PM and PMPB are computed from the primary stresses only, so excluding thermal stresses. If TABL_SIGM_THER is given, the result given in TABL_RESU_MECA is assumed to come from a thermomechanical calculation, and the thermal stresses are therefore subtracted from it. If only TABL_RESU_MECA is given, the calculation uses the stresses in the table directly.

#### 9.1.4 Option SN

Option that computes the level A criteria (excluding fatigue), which aim to protect the equipment against progressive deformation. They need the calculation of the range of linearized stress at a point, written Sn.

If the user asks for it (operand TABL_SIGM_THER present), Sn* is also computed.

If the user asks for it (operands TABL_SIGM_THER and TABL_RESU_PRES present), thermal ratcheting is also computed.

The operands needed are MATER and the stress table TABL_RESU_MECA (built by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE after the mechanical calculation on the post-processing location), given under the keyword TRANSITOIRE.

The calculation points are the two ends of the analysis segment. If several extraction segments were used to define the same stress table, the calculation is done for each of them in turn.

The limit value of Sn is 3*Sm, Sm being the allowable stress, a function of the material and the temperature, given by the keyword SM of the factor keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01].

Remark:

The keyword TABL_RESU_MECA can be repeated several times under a single keyword TRANSITOIRE . For the calculation of Sn and Sn*, however, the situations defined this way are not combined: each stress table is processed in turn.

##### 9.1.4.1 Calculation of Sn*

If the operand TABL_SIGM_THER of the factor keyword TRANSITOIRE is present, Sn* is also computed.

For the calculation to be consistent and to comply with the RCC-M, the stresses given in TABL_SIGM_THER must have been obtained with a thermal load alone, knowing that the result given by TABL_RESU_MECA may come from a combination of this thermal load with other loads. The times of the table TABL_SIGM_THER must therefore match those of the table TABL_RESU_MECA.

##### 9.1.4.2 Calculation of thermal ratcheting

If the operands TABL_SIGM_THER and TABL_RESU_PRES of the factor keyword TRANSITOIRE are present, thermal ratcheting is also computed. For this, the conventional yield strength at the maximum temperature reached during the cycle must also have been defined, either with the operand SY_MAX of POST_RCCM, or with the operand SY_02 of the keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01]. If no yield strength is defined, thermal ratcheting cannot be computed.

<!-- page 45 -->

For the calculation to be consistent and to comply with the RCC-M, the stresses given in TABL_RESU_PRES must have been obtained with a pressure load alone.

The result table shows, for each end of each analysis segment, the yield strength SY, the range of the thermal stress SP_THER, the maximum of the general membrane stress due to pressure SIGM_M_PRES, and two maximum allowable values of the range of the thermal stress, computed either assuming a linear temperature variation through the wall (VALE_MAXI_LINE) or assuming a parabolic temperature variation through the wall (VALE_MAXI_PARAB).

#### 9.1.5 Option FATIGUE_ZH210

Option that computes the usage factor resulting from the combination of one or more transients, following the method of RCC-M appendix ZH210.

The stress range at each end of the analysis segment is computed from the stress tables TABL_RESU_MECA, for each combination of times belonging to the transient or transients defined by the user. A combination and cumulation method is then applied to get the total usage factor, see [R7.04.03].

The times corresponding to the extreme states must be given by the user with the operands NUME_ORDRE, INST or LIST_INST.

Remarks:

The keyword TABL_RESU_MECA can be repeated several times under a single keyword TRANSITOIRE . For the fatigue calculation, the results held in each stress table are combined with each other.

For the operands NUME_ORDRE , INST or LIST_INS , a possible confusion is to use the list of times of the thermomechanical calculations ( MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE) ) instead of the list of times corresponding to the extreme states expected by the operator POST_RCCM . In the first case, all the calculation times are treated as stress extrema, which leads to calculation times that can be long.

<!-- chunk 26 fr=961965d66372 method=machine source=session:task-5.2 on=2026-10-30 -->

#### 9.1.6 Option AMORCAGE

Option that computes the initiation factor in a singular zone. For this option, the stresses are given as input in the table TABL_SIGM_THETA and must correspond to the extraction of the stresses, in the local coordinate system, on a circular cut line of diameter D_AMORC (a material parameter defined in the RCC-M) around the geometric singularity. The stress tables can be created with the operator MACR_LIGN_COUPE (TYPE=’ARC’).

For this option, the material properties (keyword RCCM) must also define the coefficients of the initiation law (A_AMORC and B_AMORC), the diameter of the circle on which the stresses are extracted (D_AMORC) and the coefficient between stress and effective stress (R_AMORC).

### 9.2 Operand TYPE_KE

```text
◊   TYPE_KE     =     / ‘KE_MECA’,                  [DEFAUT]
                      / ‘KE_MIXTE’
```

The elastoplastic correction factor Ke can be computed in two ways [R7.04.03]:

- KE_MECA : the original method, the only one available in versions before 7.2;
- KE_MIXTE : this method splits the range of the alternating stresses into a thermal part and a mechanical part. It has been allowed since the 1997 amendment of the RCC-M.

#### 9.3.2 Operand TABL_RESU_MECA


Table of stresses on the analysis segment, built for example by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE from mechanical results of type `evol_elas` and `evol_noli`.

#### 9.3.4 Operand TABL_SIGM_THETA

Table of stresses on the analysis segment, built for example by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE on a result obtained with the pressure load alone. This keyword is used to compute thermal ratcheting [§9.1.4.2].

##### 9.3.7.1 Warning about the list of analysis times

When post-processing stress calculations to check the RCC-M B3200 criteria, two lists of calculations must be distinguished.

In the stress calculation, the user must give a first list of calculation times, with time steps fine enough to make sure that all the extreme stress values during the transient are computed.

For the post-processing with the operator POST_RCCM, a second list of times, given by the user with the operands TOUT_ORDRE, TOUT_INST, INST or LIST_INST and distinct from the previous list of computed times, gives the times corresponding to the extreme states expected by the operator POST_RCCM .

Confusing these two lists is a frequent error. If the same list of times is given, all the calculation times are treated as stress extrema. This leads to calculation times that can be long and to excessive usage factor values.

<!-- chunk 27 fr=609ad8e0a551 method=machine source=session:task-5.2 on=2026-10-30 -->

### 9.4 Execution phase

The command checks that the calculation times of the table tabth are the same as those of the table tabmeca. It cannot, however, check that the thermal transients that contributed to the mechanical results tabth and tabmeca are the same. The user must make sure they are consistent (including the material data).

Remark:

If the tables given as input hold the coordinates of the points, consistency checks are done: alignment of the points (for the options PM_PB , SN and FATIGUE_ZH210 ) or diameter of the circular cut line (option AMORCAGE ); consistency between the different situations. It is therefore strongly recommended not to remove the point coordinates from the tables of collected stresses.

### 9.5 Example of use

An example of use of the operator POST_RCCM with results of type EVOLUTION can be found in the test case rccm01.

A calculation of the RCCM criteria goes as follows:

1) definition of the material parameters and of the fatigue curve, 2) definition of the mechanical and thermal loads, 3) linear or nonlinear thermomechanical calculation, 4) (if Sn* is computed) mechanical calculation with the thermal load alone, 5) (if thermal ratcheting is computed) mechanical calculation with pressure alone, 6) definition of the analysis segment and extraction of the results with POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE, then (possibly in a continuation):

<!-- page 48 -->

```text
SN1=POST_RCCM(MATER=MAT,
              TYPE_RESU='VALE_MAX',
              TYPE_RESU_MECA='EVOLUTION',
              OPTION='SN',
              TITRE='SN, RESULTAT: RESU2b AVEC RESUTH',
              TRANSITOIRE=_F(TABL_RESU_MECA = T_RESU2b,
                             TABL_SIGM_THER = T_RESUTHb, )
              )
IMPR_TABLE ( TABLE = sn1 )
```

An example of use of the operator POST_RCCM with results of type EVOLUTION for the option AMORCAGE can be found in the test case rccm09.

For more information, see the documents [U2.09.03] and [R7.04.03].
