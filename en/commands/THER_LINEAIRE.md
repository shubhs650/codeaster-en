## Rules

From the catalog `regles` (meanings from `code_aster/Cata/Language/Rules.py`):

- `ETAT_INIT`: exactly one of `STAT`, `EVOL_THER`, `CHAM_NO`, `VALE` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
- `INCREMENT`: at most one of `NUME_INST_INIT`, `INST_INIT` may be given (`EXCLUS`).
- `INCREMENT`: at most one of `NUME_INST_FIN`, `INST_FIN` may be given (`EXCLUS`).
- when `equal_to("PRE_COND", 'HPDDM')`: exactly one of `SEUIL`, `NB_MODE` must be given; if none is given, `NB_MODE=30` is assumed (`UN_PARMI`).
- `ARCHIVAGE`: exactly one of `PAS_ARCH`, `LIST_INST`, `INST` must be given; if none is given, `PAS_ARCH=1` is assumed (`UN_PARMI`).
