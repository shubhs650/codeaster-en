- when `is_type("RESULTAT") in (dyna_harmo,dyna_trans)`: at least one of `CONTRAINTE`, `DEFORMATION`, `ENERGIE`, `CRITERES`, `VARI_INTERNE`, `PROPRIETES`, `ACOUSTIQUE`, `FORCE`, `CHAM_UTIL` must be given (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
