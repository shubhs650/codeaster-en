## Rules

- command: at least one of `EVOL_CHAR`, `TEMP_IMPO`, `SOURCE`, `FLUX_REP`, `ECHANGE`, `ECHANGE_PAROI`, `PRE_GRAD_TEMP`, `LIAISON_DDL`, `LIAISON_GROUP`, `LIAISON_UNIF`, `LIAISON_CHAMNO`, `RAYONNEMENT`, `LIAISON_MAIL`, `CONVECTION` must be given (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
- `TEMP_IMPO`: at least one of `TOUT`, `GROUP_MA`, `GROUP_NO` must be given; if none is given, `TOUT='OUI'` is assumed (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
- `TEMP_IMPO`: at least one of `TEMP`, `TEMP_MIL`, `TEMP_SUP`, `TEMP_INF` must be given (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
- `FLUX_REP`: exactly one of `TOUT`, `GROUP_MA` must be given; if none is given, `TOUT='OUI'` is assumed (`UN_PARMI`).
- `FLUX_REP`: if `CARA_TORSION` is given, `GROUP_MA` must all be given (`PRESENT_PRESENT`).
- `FLUX_REP`: at least one of `FLUN`, `FLUN_INF`, `FLUN_SUP`, `CARA_TORSION` must be given (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
- `RAYONNEMENT`: exactly one of `TOUT`, `GROUP_MA` must be given; if none is given, `TOUT='OUI'` is assumed (`UN_PARMI`).
- `ECHANGE`: exactly one of `TOUT`, `GROUP_MA` must be given; if none is given, `TOUT='OUI'` is assumed (`UN_PARMI`).
- `ECHANGE`: at least one of `COEF_H`, `COEF_H_INF`, `COEF_H_SUP` must be given (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
- `ECHANGE`: `COEF_H`, `TEMP_EXT` are given all together or not at all (`ENSEMBLE`).
- `ECHANGE`: `COEF_H_INF`, `TEMP_EXT_INF` are given all together or not at all (`ENSEMBLE`).
- `ECHANGE`: `COEF_H_SUP`, `TEMP_EXT_SUP` are given all together or not at all (`ENSEMBLE`).
- `SOURCE`: exactly one of `SOUR`, `SOUR_CALCULEE` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
- `SOURCE`: if `TOUT` is given, none of `GROUP_MA` may be given (`PRESENT_ABSENT`).
- `SOURCE`: if `SOUR_CALCULEE` is given, none of `TOUT`, `GROUP_MA` may be given (`PRESENT_ABSENT`).
- `PRE_GRAD_TEMP`: exactly one of `TOUT`, `GROUP_MA` must be given; if none is given, `TOUT='OUI'` is assumed (`UN_PARMI`).
- `PRE_GRAD_TEMP`: at least one of `FLUX_X`, `FLUX_Y`, `FLUX_Z` must be given (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
- `LIAISON_GROUP`: exactly one of `GROUP_MA_1`, `GROUP_NO_1` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
- `LIAISON_GROUP`: exactly one of `GROUP_MA_2`, `GROUP_NO_2` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
- `LIAISON_GROUP`: at most one of `GROUP_MA_1`, `GROUP_NO_2` may be given (`EXCLUS`).
- `LIAISON_GROUP`: at most one of `GROUP_NO_1`, `GROUP_MA_2` may be given (`EXCLUS`).
- `LIAISON_MAIL`: at least one of `GROUP_MA_ESCL`, `GROUP_NO_ESCL` must be given (`AU_MOINS_UN`).
- `ECHANGE_PAROI`: exactly one of `GROUP_MA_1`, `FISSURE` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
- `ECHANGE_PAROI`: exactly one of `GROUP_MA_2`, `FISSURE` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
- when `exists("FISSURE")`: exactly one of `COEF_H`, `TEMP_CONTINUE` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
- `LIAISON_UNIF`: exactly one of `GROUP_NO`, `GROUP_MA` must be given (`UN_PARMI`).
